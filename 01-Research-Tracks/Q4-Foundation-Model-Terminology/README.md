# Q4 — Foundation-Model Terminology and Public Definitions

**Status:** Proposed; no research has started under this track.

## Working question

How do authoritative public technical and research sources define and use the term “foundation model,” and where does usage vary across disciplines and institutions?

## Initial method

Limit the inquiry to public definitions, source histories, and documented usage. Record whether each source is definitional, descriptive, taxonomic, or informal. Report variation rather than forcing a single universal definition.

## Boundaries

This track must not infer or reconstruct confidential BAES internal specifications from the name of a layer or from public terminology. It is low priority and may remain deferred.
