# Research Program Charter

**Status:** Initial charter  
**Owner:** Program Founder  
**Repository:** BAES-Research-Program

## 1. Mission

Coordinate independent, evidence-based research into the engineering and governance questions that arise when humans and AI systems jointly perform engineering work. The program examines concepts and practices such as human intent, authority, delegation, execution, evidence, decisions, traceability, oversight, and accountability, without presuming that any particular framework is effective or superior.

## 2. Relationship to BAES

The program is related to the research context surrounding the Babak AI Engineering Standard (BAES), but it is not itself a BAES layer, model, profile, policy, compliance mechanism, or amendment.

Research may examine public BAES statements alongside external frameworks and academic literature. Such examination does not confer validation, adoption, certification, endorsement, or operational status on BAES.

Only public BAES materials may be used in this repository. Confidential BAES material is excluded.

## 3. Research independence

The program must preserve:
- independence of research questions and findings;
- transparent source selection and scope;
- explicit limitations and uncertainty;
- the possibility of unfavorable or inconclusive findings;
- separation between research conclusions and decisions about BAES itself.

No sponsor, partner, or external framework owner may predetermine findings or control the research conclusions.

## 4. Program outputs

Potential outputs include:
- literature reviews and evidence maps;
- bounded analyses of public standards and frameworks;
- research protocols and preregistration records;
- scenario-based studies and empirical reports;
- assurance-case analyses;
- source registers, decision records, and limitations statements.

A proposed output is not evidence that the underlying research has been completed.

## 5. Scope boundaries

The program does not:
- modify BAES repositories;
- reconstruct confidential BAES specifications;
- claim legal or regulatory conformity;
- claim framework superiority from narrow text mappings;
- treat conceptual similarity as equivalence;
- treat a researcher's own review as independent validation;
- convert incomplete evidence into positive or negative findings.

## 6. Repository constraint

No new repository is to be created for BAES-related work. Each new project must be assigned to an appropriate existing repository before substantive work begins. This repository coordinates the wider research program; bounded projects may remain in their existing dedicated repositories and be referenced here.

## 7. Authority

The Founder retains final authority over program priorities, scope, publication, external commitments, and repository changes. Research collaborators may propose work and provide analysis but may not silently change the program's scope or publish on its behalf.
