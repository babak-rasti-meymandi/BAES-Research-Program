## Source-status notes

### NIST AI RMF 1.0

- The full NIST AI 100-1 PDF is publicly accessible.
- NIST's current AI RMF resource page, checked 2026-11-07, states that AI RMF 1.0 is being updated and a revised version is in progress. Q1 therefore treats 1.0 as a historical fixed edition and records this status to prevent an outdated “latest framework” claim.
- The NIST publication page identifies AI RMF 1.0 as published on 2023-01-26 and describes it as a voluntary, rights-preserving, non-sector-specific, use-case-agnostic resource for organizations designing, developing, deploying, or using AI systems.

### ISO/IEC 42001:2023

- ISO and IEC official public records agree on the standard's identity, first edition, December 2023 publication, stated AIMS purpose, and 51-page length.
- The ISO page identifies an Online Browsing Platform preview; the availability of a preview is not evidence that all normative clauses are publicly accessible.
- No full final-edition normative text has been examined for this Q1 source register. ISO clause-level findings remain outside the current evidence boundary.

## Register rules

1. Record the exact edition and the material actually examined.
2. A public catalogue page or abstract supports only the claims it states; it is not a substitute for normative text.
3. Record secondary sources separately and never present them as official source text.
4. Update access status when a source changes or a new authorized source becomes available.
5. Do not infer missing content from a crosswalk, preview gap, title, or page count.
