# Research Roadmap

**Status:** Proposed program sequence; no track is automatically authorized by inclusion here.

## 1. Operating principle

Prioritize work by research value, evidence readiness, duplication risk, and feasibility. Do not expand BAES Foundation content as a substitute for independent evaluation. The program's purpose is to investigate questions and report what the evidence supports.

## 3. Q1 non-duplication control (completed before activation)

The following prior-work distinctions were recorded in the Q1 Prior-Work Gap and Overlap Matrix before substantive Q1 extraction began:
- the historical C1–C20 criterion-conditioned mapping already performed;
- the NIST AI RMF 1.0 Core mapping and its investigator-side calibration history;
- the S01 event-evidence sufficiency analysis;
- the limited ISO/IEC 42001:2023 access status and absence of clause-level judgment;
- the broader questions that remain unanswered.

Q1 must not silently reuse the historical C1–C20 set as a universal or neutral framework comparison instrument. Any prior result used must be cited and labelled as prior work, preserving its original scope and limitations.

## 4. Suggested sequence

**Stage A — Program setup**
- Establish this charter, governance rules, roadmap, methods, and source register.
- Establish a project registry linking existing work.
- Confirm repository visibility and publication boundaries.

**Stage B — Q1 execution (active)**
- Audit prior work and define the incremental research question.
- Define separate source-handling methods for NIST and ISO.
- Set source eligibility, unit of analysis, extraction fields, and stopping rules.
- Q1 Protocol v1.0 approved on 2026-11-07; continue source extraction within its boundaries.

**Stage C — Literature and empirical evidence**
- Conduct Q2 and Q3 only after search protocols and inclusion/exclusion criteria are documented.
- Keep literature synthesis separate from empirical findings.

**Stage D — Assurance and engineering standards**
- Conduct Q6 and Q7 with explicit limits on terminology, edition, and access.

**Stage E — Synthesis**
- Integrate only findings whose methods and scope are compatible.
- Preserve unresolved conflicts and unknowns.
- Publish a limitations-first synthesis where evidence is incomplete.

## 5. Status definitions

- **Proposed:** candidate track; not yet scoped.
- **Scoping:** research question and feasibility are being defined.
- **Approved:** Founder has authorized the protocol and work start.
- **Active:** work is underway under an approved scope.
- **Paused:** work is temporarily stopped.
- **Completed:** stated outputs are complete; this does not imply validation.
- **Deferred:** intentionally postponed.
- **Closed:** no further work planned under the current track.

## 6. Change control

Any material change to priority, question, inclusion criteria, method, or interpretation rule must be entered in the Decision Register before implementation. Do not retrospectively change a method to fit observed findings.
