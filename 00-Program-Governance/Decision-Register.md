## Decision-entry rules

- Use sequential IDs.
- Record the date, decision wording, approval status, rationale, and affected repositories/tracks.
- Record rejected or superseded decisions rather than silently deleting history.
- Distinguish a Founder-approved decision from an assistant recommendation.
- Do not record a proposal as approved without explicit approval.
