# Q8 — Exploratory Idea Register

**Status:** Proposed / exploratory  
**Validation:** None established  
**Purpose:** Record candidate research questions without implying acceptance or adoption.

## Evaluation discipline

Each candidate must be examined on its own merits. For every candidate, distinguish:
- the problem it is intended to address;
- the proposed analytical construct;
- evidence that the problem occurs;
- whether the construct adds explanatory or evaluative value;
- limitations, counterexamples, and overlap;
- dependencies that are demonstrated rather than assumed;
- whether it belongs in research material, a model, a tool, or nowhere.

A candidate may be rejected, narrowed, merged after independent analysis, deferred, or left unresolved. Similarity to an existing concept is not sufficient evidence of equivalence.

## Candidate-specific cautions

### Q8-C01 — Canonical Definitions and Boundaries
Do not assume that every component requires a separate canonical definition or that a register itself resolves ambiguity. Test whether it improves consistency and boundary detection.

### Q8-C02 — Component Relationships and Dependencies
Represent relationship type, direction, scope, and evidential basis. Do not infer dependency from proximity, naming, correlation, or conceptual association. Uncertainty should transfer only through demonstrated relevant dependencies.

### Q8-C03 — Correction Lifecycle
Keep deviation detection, classification, evidence collection, human review, authorization, corrective action, verification, closure, and escalation distinct. Detection is not authorization; a proposed correction is not permission to execute it; execution is not proof of successful correction.

### Q8-C04 — Cross-Domain Semantic Mapping
Record preserved properties, non-preserved properties, domain assumptions, unmapped elements, and confidence limits. Do not force one-to-one equivalence where structures differ.

### Q8-C05 — Layered Governance Analysis
Examine oversight, policy/governance, verification, operations, audit, and accountability as potentially distinct functions. Do not equate oversight with authority or verification with authorization.

### Q8-C06 — Use-Case Classification
Test whether a compact scenario schema supports reproducible classification without suppressing context or exceptional conditions. Do not assume one schema fits every engineering domain.

### Q8-C07 — Structural Adversarial Evaluation
Potential transformation classes include renaming, semantic substitution, authority substitution, responsibility diffusion, evidence removal, delegation expansion, and simulated approval. These are candidate test classes, not a validated coverage set or guarantee of detection.

### Q8-C08 — Feedback and Decision-State Transitions
Distinguish receipt and recording of information from evaluation, approval, authorization, and state transition. Feedback alone must not be presumed to change a decision or grant permission.
