## 1. Purpose and control

This file is the operational record of the Q1 research path. It distinguishes:

- **Planned:** what the approved research design intended to do;
- **Actual:** what has been completed and recorded;
- **Revised:** what remains necessary in light of the evidence and current access conditions.

It complements, and does not replace, the program-level [Research Roadmap](../../../00-Program-Governance/Research-Roadmap.md), the [Q1 README](README.md), or the approved [Q1 Research Protocol v1.0](02-Q1-Research-Protocol-v1.0.md).

The protocol controls the research question, scope, analytical dimensions, source boundary, interpretation rules, validation, reporting, and stop conditions. This tracker records execution against that protocol; it does not authorize scope changes.

## 2. Research destination

Q1 asks what can responsibly be established, from the materials actually examined, about the native structures and stated purposes of NIST AI RMF 1.0 and ISO/IEC 42001:2023, particularly in relation to organizational AI governance, human authority and oversight, risk management, evidence/documentation, and lifecycle control.

The intended output is a bounded, source-transparent analysis. It is not a framework ranking, conformity assessment, certification assessment, or evaluation of BAES effectiveness.

### Evidence and interpretation safeguards

- Preserve each source's native structure and purpose.
- Do not treat NIST and ISO as equivalent document types or analytical units.
- Do not infer absence from inaccessible ISO text.
- Do not convert organizational oversight into a universal task-level approval requirement without direct textual support.
- Keep source statements, researcher interpretations, and unknowns distinct.
- Do not repeat or generalize the historical C1–C20 / S01 work.
- Do not use or reconstruct confidential BAES material.
- Do not produce a composite score, ranking, or BAES-superiority claim.

## 7. Q1 completion criteria

Q1 may be considered complete only when the final record:

1. Answers the approved research question to the extent supported by examined evidence.
2. Describes NIST AI RMF 1.0 in its native structure and includes source-location-verified findings relevant to the approved dimensions.
3. Describes ISO/IEC 42001:2023 only to the extent supported by official public materials and any justified, separately registered secondary sources.
4. Explicitly preserves the asymmetry of source access and all unresolved ISO unknowns.
5. Distinguishes within-framework findings from cross-framework comparisons.
6. Includes counterevidence, ambiguity, alternative interpretations, and limitations.
7. Verifies material source references and records whether independent review was obtained.
8. Discloses protocol deviations and the NIST 1.0 revision status.
9. Makes no unsupported ranking, conformity, effectiveness, task-level authorization, or BAES-superiority claim.
10. Respects repository and confidentiality boundaries.
