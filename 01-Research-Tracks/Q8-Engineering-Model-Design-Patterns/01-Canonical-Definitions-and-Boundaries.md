# Q8-C01 — Canonical Definitions and Boundaries

**Status:** Exploratory  
**Validation:** Not established

## Research question

Can a structured definition-and-boundary register improve precision when describing components of an engineering model?

## Candidate fields

A candidate record may include:
- term or component identifier;
- positive definition;
- intended function;
- scope and applicability;
- explicit exclusions and non-functions;
- inputs and outputs;
- responsibilities and constraints;
- relationships to other components;
- evidence supporting the definition;
- unresolved ambiguity and version history.

These fields are a starting hypothesis, not a mandatory schema.

## Evaluation questions

1. Does the register reduce inconsistent use of terms?
2. Can reviewers distinguish a component's function from its authority or responsibility?
3. Are exclusions useful for detecting scope creep?
4. Does the schema create unnecessary duplication or false precision?
5. Can the record remain useful when a concept is context-dependent?

## Boundaries

A definition does not itself confer authority, establish implementation permission, or prove that a component exists in a deployed system. Do not convert exploratory definitions into BAES normative language without separate approval.

## Required evidence

Use independently reviewable examples, explicit counterexamples, and documented reviewer disagreement. Record cases where the register fails to resolve ambiguity.

## Open issues

The minimum useful field set, treatment of context-dependent terms, and relationship to existing terminology registers remain undetermined.
