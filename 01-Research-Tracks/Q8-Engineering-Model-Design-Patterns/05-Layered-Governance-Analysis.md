# Q8-C05 — Layered Governance Analysis

**Status:** Exploratory  
**Validation:** Not established

## Research question

Can governance-related functions be analyzed in distinct layers without conflating oversight, authority, verification, execution, and accountability?

## Candidate analytical distinctions

- Oversight and monitoring.
- Policy and governance rules.
- Verification and assurance.
- Operational execution.
- Audit and review.
- Accountability and responsibility.

These are candidate analytical categories, not a prescribed architecture. Their boundaries and possible overlaps require examination.

## Evaluation questions

- What decision or function does each category describe?
- Who holds authority for the relevant decision?
- Which activities are advisory, controlling, evidentiary, or operational?
- Where can responsibility become diffuse?
- How are conflicts between policy, operational constraints, and human decisions handled?

## Non-equivalences

Human oversight must not be presumed equivalent to Human Authority. Verification of alignment or compliance must not be presumed equivalent to authorization. Monitoring does not itself grant permission to intervene or execute.

## Boundaries

Do not derive a governance architecture or normative BAES requirement from the classification alone. Identify contexts where the categories do not fit.
