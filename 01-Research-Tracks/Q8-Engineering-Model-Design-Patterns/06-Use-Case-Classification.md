# Q8-C06 — Use-Case Classification

**Status:** Exploratory  
**Validation:** Not established

## Research question

What minimum information is needed to classify an engineering use case while retaining the context necessary for meaningful evaluation?

## Candidate scenario fields

- Context and operating environment.
- Engineering objective and problem.
- Human intent and decision authority.
- Delegated task and execution boundary.
- Roles and responsibilities.
- Constraints and prohibited actions.
- Failure conditions and foreseeable deviations.
- Available evidence and traceability.
- Evaluation question and outcome criteria.

## Evaluation questions

1. Which fields are essential for every scenario?
2. Which fields are domain-specific or conditional?
3. Can two reviewers classify the same scenario consistently?
4. Does the schema preserve unusual but material context?
5. Does classification support comparison without flattening unlike cases?

## Boundaries

A classification schema does not determine whether a use case is safe, authorized, compliant, or acceptable. Do not treat missing information as proof that a condition is absent.

## Required evidence

Test on varied engineering scenarios, including ambiguous and incomplete descriptions. Record inter-reviewer disagreement and schema-induced information loss.
