# Q5 — AI-Assisted Engineering Failure Modes and Mitigations

**Status:** Proposed; no research has started under this track.

## Working question

What failure modes have been documented in AI-assisted engineering workflows, and what detection or mitigation mechanisms have credible supporting evidence?

## Initial method

Build a source-backed taxonomy with explicit inclusion criteria. Separate observed incidents, controlled experimental findings, practitioner reports, theoretical risks, and proposed mitigations. Track severity and likelihood only where the source supports them.

## Boundaries

Do not turn anecdotal reports into prevalence estimates. Do not assume that a proposed mitigation is effective without evaluation evidence. Keep this taxonomy distinct from the S01 scenario record.

## Required before activation

1. Define the precise scope and intended contribution.
2. Identify prior work and potential duplication.
3. Establish source eligibility and an auditable search/extraction method.
4. Record limitations, unknowns, and expected outputs.
5. Obtain explicit Founder approval before substantive research begins.
