## Source-status notes

### NIST AI RMF 1.0

- The full NIST AI 100-1 PDF is publicly accessible. The native Core inventory has been transcribed into a 19-Category / 72-Subcategory paraphrase record; a mechanical count check confirmed 19 Category headings and 72 unique Subcategory identifiers.
- NIST's current AI RMF resource page, checked 2026-11-07, states that AI RMF 1.0 is being updated and a revised version is in progress. Q1 therefore treats 1.0 as a historical fixed edition and records this status to prevent an outdated “latest framework” claim.
- The NIST publication page identifies AI RMF 1.0 as published on 2023-01-26 and describes it as a voluntary, rights-preserving, non-sector-specific, use-case-agnostic resource for organizations designing, developing, deploying, or using AI systems.

### ISO/IEC 42001:2023

- ISO and IEC official public records agree on the standard's identity, first edition, December 2023 publication, stated AIMS purpose, and 51-page length. These are publication metadata and broad public summaries only.
- The ISO copyright page and Online Browsing Platform terms state restrictions on AI/ML-related use of ISO content, with an exception referenced for material available through ISO Open Data and subject to its terms.
- On 2026-11-07, the Q1 ISO workstream was placed on **Access / Terms Blocked** status. No substantive content from the ISO/IEC 42001:2023 preview has been processed for this analysis.
- The exact applicability of the ISO Open Data exception to any relevant material has not been established. It must not be presumed.
- No full final-edition normative text has been examined. Clause-level findings remain [UNK — not examined / access-limited], not negative findings about the standard.

## Register rules

1. Record the exact edition and the material actually examined.
2. A public catalogue page or abstract supports only the claims it states; it is not a substitute for normative text.
3. Record secondary sources separately and never present them as official source text.
4. Update access status when a source changes or a new authorized source becomes available.
5. Do not infer missing content from a crosswalk, preview gap, title, or page count.
