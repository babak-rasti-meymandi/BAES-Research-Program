# Q8-C04 — Cross-Domain Semantic Mapping

**Status:** Exploratory  
**Validation:** Not established

## Research question

When can a concept from one technical or organizational domain be mapped to a concept in another domain without concealing material differences?

## Candidate mapping record

- source concept and domain;
- target concept and domain;
- mapping purpose;
- properties preserved;
- properties not preserved;
- assumptions and preconditions;
- structural differences;
- unmapped or unmatched elements;
- evidence and confidence limits;
- known counterexamples.

## Evaluation questions

1. Is the mapping terminological, functional, structural, causal, or analogical?
2. Which properties are invariant under the mapping?
3. Which properties are lost or transformed?
4. Does the mapping hold only under specific conditions?
5. Can an independent reviewer identify where the analogy breaks?

## Boundaries

A semantic mapping is not proof of equivalence, interchangeability, or transferability. Do not import domain-specific authority, obligations, or safety claims through analogy alone.

## Required evidence

Use cases where a mapping appears plausible, cases where it fails, and cases where only partial correspondence can be justified.
