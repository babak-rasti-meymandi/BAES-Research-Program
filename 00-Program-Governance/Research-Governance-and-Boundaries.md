# Research Governance and Boundaries

**Status:** Initial control document

## 1. Authority and approval

The Founder retains final authority over:
- starting, pausing, narrowing, or closing research tracks;
- changing research questions, methods, or evaluation criteria;
- approving public release and external communication;
- accepting sponsorship or collaboration conditions;
- changes to BAES repositories.

A track's appearance on the roadmap is planning information, not authorization to begin it.

## 2. Repository boundaries

| Repository | Permitted role in this program | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| BAES-Research-Program | Program governance, research plans, methods, source registers, cross-track synthesis, and public research outputs | No confidential BAES content; no duplication of detailed project records without a reason |
| Human-AI-Authorization-Evaluation | Existing bounded authorization and semantic-deviation evaluation, including S01 and its associated evidence and historical comparative work | Keep its scenario-specific methods and findings in that project; link from here |
| Human-AI-Authorization-Evaluation-Private | Private working material for the companion evaluation project | Never copy private material into this public repository |
| BAES-Public | Public BAES standard and related public material | No modification through this program; separate explicit approval required |
| BAES-SECRET | Confidential BAES material | Frozen/protected; no access, modification, disclosure, quotation, reconstruction, or derivative publication through this program |

This table describes working boundaries, not a grant of access or permission to modify any repository.

## 3. Public-source rule

Use sources that are publicly accessible and lawfully usable. Record the exact edition, version, publication date, URL, access date, and examined portion where available. Do not imply access to the full text of a paywalled or licensed standard when only a preview, abstract, catalogue entry, or secondary crosswalk was examined.

## 4. Evidence-status vocabulary

Use these labels consistently:
- **[OBS] Observation:** directly visible in a cited source or artifact.
- **[EXT] External source statement:** a factual statement about an external source, supported by that source.
- **[INF] Inference:** a reasoned interpretation from identified observations.
- **[HYP] Hypothesis:** a proposition to be tested, not treated as established.
- **[UNK] Unknown:** not established by the examined material.
- **[NA] Not applicable:** outside the defined scope for a stated reason.

Do not label an inference as a fact. Do not use [UNK] as evidence of absence.

## 5. Research integrity

- Preserve negative, mixed, and inconclusive findings.
- Do not alter criteria or ratings merely to improve the apparent position of BAES or another framework.
- Version changes to criteria and methods; retain the prior version and explain the reason for any change.
- Separate text coverage, event evidence sufficiency, implementation, and effectiveness.
- Do not aggregate unlike dimensions into a score or ranking without a justified, pre-specified method.
- Distinguish investigator-side review from independent review and human-subject empirical findings.
- Record conflicts of interest, sponsor involvement, and material deviations from protocol.

## 6. Publication and confidentiality gate

Before publication, check:
1. whether every source is public and lawfully reusable in the proposed form;
2. whether any confidential BAES material or private working content is present;
3. whether claims are supported by cited evidence;
4. whether limitations and source-access asymmetries are explicit;
5. whether planned work is incorrectly described as completed;
6. whether third-party, privacy, licensing, or security concerns remain.

If any boundary is uncertain, pause publication and ask the Founder.

## 7. External collaboration

Funding or collaboration must not grant a sponsor authority to alter research questions, criteria, methods, findings, or publication conclusions. Any proposed agreement must be reviewed separately before commitment. No financial or institutional commitment is created by this document.
