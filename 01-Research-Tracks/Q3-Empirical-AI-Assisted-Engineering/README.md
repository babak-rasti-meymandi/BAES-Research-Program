# Q3 — Empirical AI-Assisted Engineering Research

**Status:** Proposed; no research has started under this track.

## Working question

What empirical evidence exists about AI-assisted engineering outcomes and process risks, particularly intent preservation, authorization, delegation, review, traceability, and human decision-making?

## Initial method

First map existing empirical studies and datasets. For any proposed new study, define the population, task, comparator, outcome measures, preregistration, ethics/privacy requirements, and feasibility before recruitment or data collection.

## Boundaries

Do not imply that existing S01 investigator-side documentary analysis is a human-subject study or evidence of reviewer performance. New human participation requires a separate approved protocol.

## Required before activation

1. Define the precise scope and intended contribution.
2. Identify prior work and potential duplication.
3. Establish source eligibility and an auditable search/extraction method.
4. Record limitations, unknowns, and expected outputs.
5. Obtain explicit Founder approval before substantive research begins.

## Outputs

To be specified in the approved track protocol. A proposed track is not a completed study or a commitment to conduct one.
