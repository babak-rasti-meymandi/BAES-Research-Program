# Q7 — Systems and Software Engineering Standards

**Status:** Proposed; no research has started under this track.

## Working question

What relevant concepts concerning lifecycle processes, requirements, traceability, risk, responsibility, and human/system roles appear in selected systems and software engineering standards?

## Initial method

Select standards by a stated relevance rationale. Record edition, scope, official public descriptions, accessible clauses or previews, and secondary sources. Respect each standard's own structure; do not force clause equivalence across standards.

## Boundaries

Do not claim complete clause-level coverage when full authorized text is unavailable. Distinguish official source text from secondary summaries and crosswalks. No standard is treated as a normative BAES template.

## Required before activation

1. Define the precise scope and intended contribution.
2. Identify prior work and potential duplication.
3. Establish source eligibility and an auditable search/extraction method.
4. Record limitations, unknowns, and expected outputs.
5. Obtain explicit Founder approval before substantive research begins.

## Outputs

To be specified in the approved track protocol. A proposed track is not a completed study or a commitment to conduct one.
