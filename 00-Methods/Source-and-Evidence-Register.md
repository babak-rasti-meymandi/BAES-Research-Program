## 3. Program-level source entries

No new external source has been independently verified for a new track in this initial setup. Add entries only when a source has actually been inspected and its relevant details recorded.

## 4. Integrity rule

A source being listed here does not mean that its full contents were examined, that it supports every claim associated with its topic, or that its conclusions have been independently verified.
