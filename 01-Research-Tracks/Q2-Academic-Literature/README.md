# Q2 — Academic Literature Review

**Status:** Proposed; no research has started under this track.

## Working question

What does the peer-reviewed and otherwise methodologically credible literature establish about separation of duties, least privilege, delegation, meaningful human control, levels of automation, responsibility gaps, and assurance in human–AI or automated engineering contexts?

## Initial method

Review literature using a documented search strategy, inclusion/exclusion criteria, screening log, and evidence-quality appraisal. Distinguish conceptual papers, empirical studies, legal/philosophical arguments, and surveys. Do not treat citation counts or publication venue alone as evidence quality.

## Boundaries

Do not present a literature review as empirical validation of BAES. Avoid treating adjacent concepts as equivalent to BAES concepts. Include contradictory findings and gaps.

## Required before activation

1. Define the precise scope and intended contribution.
2. Identify prior work and potential duplication.
3. Establish source eligibility and an auditable search/extraction method.
4. Record limitations, unknowns, and expected outputs.
5. Obtain explicit Founder approval before substantive research begins.
