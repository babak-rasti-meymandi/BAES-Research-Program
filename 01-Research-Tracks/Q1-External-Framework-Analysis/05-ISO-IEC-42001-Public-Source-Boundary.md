## 1. Document identity and previously established public metadata

**[EXT — previously recorded from official catalogue/product records]** ISO and IEC public publication records identify ISO/IEC 42001:2023 as the first edition of *Information technology — Artificial intelligence — Management system*, published in December 2023, with a stated length of 51 pages.

**[EXT — previously recorded public summaries]** The official catalogue/product descriptions characterize the publication broadly as specifying requirements and providing guidance for establishing, implementing, maintaining, and continually improving an AI management system within an organization.

These statements are limited to public publication metadata and summaries previously recorded. They are not clause-level findings and are not used to infer the content of inaccessible normative provisions.

## 2. Access and terms restriction

**[EXT — publisher terms]** The restriction is the reason this Q1 workstream is paused. The ISO preview is not to be opened, inspected, extracted, summarized, compared, or otherwise processed by AI for this research unless a clearly applicable permission or exception is established first.

**[OBS — Q1 handling]** No substantive content from the ISO/IEC 42001:2023 preview has been processed for this analysis. The earlier plan to inspect the preview is superseded by this access/terms stop.
