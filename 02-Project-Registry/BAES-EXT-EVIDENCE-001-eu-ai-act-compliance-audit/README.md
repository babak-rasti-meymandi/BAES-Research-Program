# BAES-EXT-EVIDENCE-001 — External Regulatory Evidence: EU AI Act Compliance Audit

**Status:** Planned — source pending  
**Classification:** Public-source regulatory and engineering research  
**BAES integration:** Not established

## Purpose

Assess whether a summarized EU AI Act compliance-audit report provides reliable, relevant evidence for examining Human–AI Engineering Interaction concepts and evidence requirements.

The project will distinguish:
- requirements stated in the EU AI Act and other primary legal sources;
- interpretations made by the audit report's author;
- technical or organizational recommendations;
- possible connections to existing BAES concepts;
- candidate concepts or gaps that require separate justification.

## Current evidence state

The project was initiated from a secondary summary supplied in conversation. The underlying audit report has not yet been identified or verified. No legal claim in that summary is treated as validated at this stage.

**Source status:** Original report, author/publisher, publication date, methodology, and cited legal provisions are pending identification and verification.

## Research sequence

1. Identify and obtain the original audit report and its cited sources.
2. Extract its material claims without treating the report's conclusions as law.
3. Verify legal claims against authoritative EU primary sources, including the applicable consolidated legal text and relevant official guidance where appropriate.
4. Separate binding legal requirements from guidance, standards, implementation practices, and the report author's recommendations.
5. Build a claim-to-source evidence matrix.
6. Map verified claims to existing BAES concepts without modifying the frozen Foundation.
7. Classify each relationship as existing coverage, supporting evidence, potential extension, new candidate, or out of scope.
8. Record limitations, unresolved questions, and findings that remain not established.

## Initial claim areas to verify

The supplied summary refers to human oversight (Article 14), transparency and disclosure (Article 50), logging (Article 12), accuracy/robustness/cybersecurity (Article 15), technical documentation (Annex IV), post-market monitoring (Article 72), risk management, data governance, provider/deployer responsibilities, conformity assessment, and retention periods.

These are research leads only. Their applicability, wording, scope, actor allocation, exceptions, and effective dates must be checked against primary sources. In particular, any generalized claim about a six-month log-retention period or a continuously displayed notice must not be adopted without precise legal support and scope analysis.

## Evidence matrix

Each material claim should be recorded with at least:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Claim ID | Stable identifier |
| Report claim | Faithful, concise statement from the audit report |
| Claim type | Legal text / interpretation / recommendation / technical assertion |
| Primary source | Exact article, paragraph, annex, official guidance, or other source |
| Applicability | Relevant system, actor, risk category, and conditions |
| BAES concept | Relevant existing concept, if any |
| Relationship | Existing / Supporting Evidence / Potential Extension / New Candidate / Out of Scope |
| Evidence and reasoning | Support for the mapping |
| Limitations | Uncertainty, exceptions, missing evidence, or contrary evidence |
| Validation status | Pending / Verified / Partially verified / Not established |

## Boundaries

- This project is a research analysis, not legal advice or a conformity assessment.
- It does not presume that the EU AI Act is a general-purpose engineering standard.
- It does not convert jurisdiction-specific legal duties into universal BAES requirements.
- It does not modify or expand the frozen BAES Foundation.
- It remains separate from the S01 semantic-deviation evaluation; a comparison may be made after the independent legal-source analysis.
- No confidential BAES material is to be included in this public repository.

## Initial deliverable

A source-verified claim matrix and a concise analytical report stating what is supported, partially supported, not established, or outside scope.

**Next action:** Identify the original audit report before evaluating its summary claims.
